Get started with MontClip

Installer in preparation

There is no ready-to-install version yet. When it is ready, it will be posted on this page with its version number and supported systems.

System requirements

Tested on Windows 11 only so far. macOS and Linux have not been tested.

Where your recordings go

Importing, editing and exporting all happen on your computer. Recordings are not uploaded.

FAQ

How is MontClip related to FableCut?

MontClip is a modified version of FableCut and keeps its MIT license. For FableCut's full feature set, see the upstream FableCut documentation; MontClip has not verified those features one by one.

Can I cut Chinese by individual words?

Not yet. Chinese transcription currently has sentence-level timing only, so you can cut by sentence. Word-level cutting is still being verified.

Do I need to sign in or register?

Not for editing. The AI assistant is still planned; you will only need your own AI service account once you use it.
