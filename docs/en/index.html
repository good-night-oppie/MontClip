MontClip

Turn stream recordings into clips. Pick by sentence, then export.

A modified version of FableCut (MIT).

View source Get started

What it can do today

Import local recordings

Add video files from your computer to a project. Recordings are processed on your computer.

Keep by sentence

Tick the subtitle sentences you want to keep and MontClip joins them in order. Preview, then apply; one Undo reverts it, and your original files are never changed.

Export MP4 and subtitles

Export the finished clip as MP4, with a matching SRT subtitle file.

Optional AI assistant

Connect your own AI service account and ask it for editing suggestions. You see the text before it is sent; nothing changes until you click Apply, and it can be undone. Charges are billed to your own account. Editing works fine without it.

About the upstream project

MontClip is a modified version of FableCut and keeps its MIT license. FableCut has many more features that MontClip has not verified one by one, so they are not listed here.
